## 🎯 What Is dsh-lowtide?

dsh-lowtide is a friendly helper app that lets you **plan your tasks whenever you want**, and then it **runs them for you automatically** when your computer is not busy. Think of it like a smart assistant that works the night shift while you sleep.

You write down what you need done, set a time, and walk away. When you come back, dsh-lowtide has a clean report waiting for you. No need to watch, wait, or click anything.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

No. Everything is point-and-click. If you can use a web browser, you can use dsh-lowtide.

Yes. All your tasks and reports stay on your own computer. Nothing is sent to external servers except what you explicitly share with dsh.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### The app does not open
Make sure you extracted the ZIP file completely. Try right-clicking the EXE file and selecting "Run as administrator".

### I cannot connect to dsh
Double-check the address and port number. Make sure dsh is running before you start dsh-lowtide.

### Tasks are not running at the scheduled time
Check that your computer is awake and not in sleep mode. You can adjust power settings to prevent sleep during scheduled tasks.

### The report is empty
This usually means the task failed silently. Check the "Logs" tab in the app for error messages. Most issues are fixed by checking the dsh connection.
